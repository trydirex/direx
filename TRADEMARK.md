# The Direx name and logo

DRAFT. No lawyer has reviewed this text, and the marks are not registered. It becomes final
together with [CLA.md](CLA.md).

The code in this repository is open source. The name is not part of that. "Direx" and the Direx
logo belong to KD Partners LTD, and the AGPL and MIT licenses here give you no right to use them beyond
what this page says.

## What you can do without asking

- Use the name to talk about the project: in an article, a talk, a review or a comparison.
- Say in plain words that your product works with Direx, is built on it or is a plugin for it.
  "Acme for Direx" is a fine name for a plugin.
- Run Direx unchanged for yourself or your company, and call it Direx.

## When you need your own name

- **A changed version you give to others.** Give it its own name and logo. You can say it is
  based on Direx.
- **A hosted service you offer to others.** Don't call it Direx, and don't put Direx in its name
  or its domain. You can say it runs Direx.

## What you can't do

- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests KD Partners LTD or the Direx project made, supports or approves of your
  product.
- Register a company, domain, trademark or social account with Direx in its name. A plugin's
  product name such as "Acme for Direx" is fine; a domain or a company named after it is not.
- Change the logo, or make it part of your own.

## Asking

If your case isn't covered here, email dominik@trydirex.com before you use the name.
