Which checks this skill makes, and four of its rules, take their ideas from claude-seo
(https://github.com/AgricIDaniel/claude-seo) at commit `6be93f45064ffbadced572b5d5c95f55fd263af0`,
`agents/seo-agentic.md`: report training, search and user-triggered access separately; a missing
llms.txt or Markdown version is an opportunity, not a defect; never promise ranking, citation or
traffic effects; check whether the page's content is in the HTML as served.
